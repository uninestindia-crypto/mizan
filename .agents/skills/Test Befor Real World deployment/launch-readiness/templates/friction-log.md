## The rules the observer must follow

1. **Say nothing.** No explaining, no hinting, no "you're nearly there".
2. **Do not touch the keyboard or the mouse.** Not even to unstick them.
3. Ask them to narrate their thinking out loud. Prompt only with "what are you thinking?"
4. **Let them fail.** The failure is the finding. Failing is the point of the session.
5. Record the time at every friction point.
6. Write down what they *say*, in their own words, not your interpretation of it.

**The moment you explain something, you have learned the thing and destroyed the evidence.** Every
explanation you want to give is a change the product needs.

## If no human was available

State it plainly so the report is honest:

> **No human participant was available.** A fresh agent session was used instead, given only the
> URL and the goal, with no context about how the product was built. This is meaningfully weaker
> than a human session — an agent does not get frustrated, does not have a real motivation, and
> will persist far past the point where a person would leave. Treat every finding below as a lower
> bound on the real friction.
