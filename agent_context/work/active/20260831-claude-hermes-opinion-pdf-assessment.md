### Why #4 is not a hyperparameter change

`MIZAN_CANONICAL_WINDOW_BARS = 400` is a **correctness boundary, not a modelling choice**. Wilder RSI
is an EMA with `alpha = 1/14`, so start-of-series influence decays as `(13/14)^n` and never reaches
zero. The module docstring records the measurement: 51 bars moves `rsi_14_centered` by up to **0.246
of its own standard deviation**, 400 bars moves it by **1.71e-12**. The number exists so that
training (which fed an expanding prefix) and execution (which feeds a bounded window) compute the
same value for the same decision bar.

Making that window "adaptive" reintroduces precisely the window-dependence defect that
`agent_context/decisions/20260824-canonical-feature-window.md` exists to prevent, and that
`20260826-NOTICE-mizan-v3-reintroduces-window-dependence.md` was filed about. The document reads a
safety invariant as a tunable and proposes tuning it.

### Why #1 and #6 are self-defeating, which is the document's deepest error

The document treats the model as the free variable and the promotion gate as fixed. It is the other
way round. The Sharpe required to clear `min_deflated_sharpe = 0.95`
(`.launch/RESEARCH-DESIGN-BRIEF-NEXT-CAMPAIGN.md:44-49`):

| validation periods | N=3 trials | N=51 trials | N=110 trials |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 63 | 5.12 | **8.13** | 8.73 |
| 504 | **1.77** | 2.79 | 2.99 |

Validation length and trial economy move the bar by a factor of **4.6**. No feature engineering has
ever delivered anything close. Adding model families, comparing them on the same fold, and then
stacking them all **spend multiplicity ordinals**, which raises the bar they are meant to clear.
Recommendation #6 proposes making promotion harder as a route to promotion.

- **`india_vix_level` is absent from the output entirely.** Fifteen features are declared; fourteen

  error demonstrated rather than asserted.
- **Two feature pairs are exactly duplicated.** `return_5` and `cs_rank_momentum_5` return identical

  the same column computed twice. That is the collinearity finding, made concrete.

  estimators are insignificant, and the strongest signals are the two longest-lookback trend
  features. Consistent with mean reversion at this horizon, and consistent across all eight.
